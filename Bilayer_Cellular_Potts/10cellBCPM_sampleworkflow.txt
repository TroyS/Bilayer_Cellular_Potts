Here is an example of initializing a 10 cell simulation and then collecting data:


mkdir 10cellexamplerun

cp BilayerCPinitupd.cpp 10cellexamplerun

cp BilayerCPbisweepupd.cpp 10cellexamplerun

cp 10cellinitexample.dat 10cellexamplerun

cp 10cellsweepexample.dat 10cellexamplerun

cd 10cellexamplerun

g++ -o bilaycpset BilayerCPinitupd.cpp

./bilaycpset 10cellinitexample.dat A1040example 0 500 10

g++ -o bilaycpbisweep BilayerCPbisweepupd.cpp

./bilaycpbisweep A1040exampleregion.dat 10cellsweepexample.dat p0_4_A1040example
